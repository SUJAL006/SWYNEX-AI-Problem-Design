Route dining-hall comments to the right owner

A single-label classifier for a small campus dining team. Not a chatbot. Not a sentiment dashboard. Four buckets, human review on the unsure cases.

Problem

Students submit short comments through a QR “How was your meal?” form. Today a coordinator reads every message and forwards it in Slack: kitchen, service, or facilities. That routing is slow, inconsistent, and eats the hour that should go to fixing the actual issue.

The model’s only job is to propose one label so the right person sees the ticket first. A person still decides what to do.

User

Primary user
Dining operations coordinator for one residential dining hall (not a system-wide analytics team).
Secondary users
Kitchen lead, service lead, and facilities lead who receive labeled tickets in an existing shared inbox.
Job to be done
Every weekday morning, clear overnight comments in under 15 minutes, with cleanliness issues never sitting unnoticed.
Success looks like
Comments land with the right owner on the first pass; the coordinator only opens low-confidence or “other” items.
